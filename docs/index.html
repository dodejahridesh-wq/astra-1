OPEN RESEARCH · ASTRA-1

What if the next generation of AI is more than a model?

Astra-1 explores the cognitive architecture around capable models: memory, world modeling, planning, verification, metacognition, skills, simulation, governance and collective cognition.

Explore the repositoryJoin the research

The loop

Goal → Perceive → Retrieve → Model → Plan → Simulate
  ↓
Act → Observe → Verify → Reflect → Consolidate → Learn

Persistent

Explicit memory and world state instead of a purely transient conversation.

Verifiable

Verification, provenance and uncertainty are first-class architecture.

Controlled

Simulation is separated from real action and consequential actions require authorization.

Astra-1 is an experimental research project. It does not claim consciousness, sentience or AGI.
