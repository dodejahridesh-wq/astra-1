## LinkedIn
We are building Astra-1, an open research platform exploring persistent cognitive AI architectures. Instead of treating the foundation model as the whole system, Astra-1 treats it as a replaceable cognitive substrate and builds explicit memory, world modeling, planning, verification, metacognition, skills, simulation and governance around it.

The first release is intentionally deterministic and inspectable. The goal is not hype; it is a public research program that can be measured, criticized and extended.
